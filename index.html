Rubik

Rubik is OneCore Tech’s operating system and intelligent operating fabric. Parts of it are built. The whole design is still in progress. It is not a production system, and it is not yet running on a device. Open review items remain, so we will not call it finished.

The aim is procedural creation of movies, games, 3D worlds, images, robotics, and diffusion. That is the destination. The road is still under construction, and we will not pretend otherwise.
