About

OneCore Tech is Joey (Jose Rosario)’s compute company, working from Marion, South Carolina. Rubik is the product.

I built everything using Claude, Gemini and Grok.

Rubik

Rubik is OneCore Tech’s operating system (Rubik OS) and intelligent operating fabric. Parts of it are built — including Pure-P OS components and a framebuffer Endgame demo verified in QEMU — while the whole design is still in progress. It is not production-ready, it is not yet running on physical hardware, and Rubik is not for sale.

The aim is procedural creation of movies, games, 3D worlds, images, robotics, and diffusion. That is the destination. The road is still under construction, and we will not pretend otherwise.
